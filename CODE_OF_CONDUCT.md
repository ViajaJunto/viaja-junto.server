## Code of Conduct

### Commitment

This project brings together contributors with different backgrounds and perspectives. Our commitment is to maintain product quality, mutual respect, and a healthy collaboration environment.

### Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Be committed to the project and its goals.
- Help others whenever possible.
- Be patient and open to questions.
- Communicate clearly and professionally.

### Unacceptable behavior

- Insults or harassment.
- Offensive communication.
- Inappropriate comments.
- Intolerance or discrimination.
- Disrespect toward people or the project community.
